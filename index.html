Case Study: The Clarity Overhaul

The KLS Solution: We audited their buyer journey and identified three "Dark Zones." We produced a suite of Digital Products—a Buyer's Guide, a Deployment Roadmap, and a Readiness Checklist—that educated the market in the founder's voice.

The Outcome: By providing these high-trust assets early in the funnel, the team saw a 40% increase in lead quality and a significant reduction in repeat questions during discovery calls.
